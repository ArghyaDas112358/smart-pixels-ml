## 1. The problem in one paragraph

A CMS smart-pixel ASIC can read **2 of 101 waveform time slices** at **2 bits
(3 comparator thresholds)** from a 16×16 pixel cluster and must regress four
track parameters (x, y, cot α, cot β) with a full 4-D Gaussian uncertainty.
The network is a small ViT (`ViT_MaxDeep_PairLattice`, ~711K params): a
**pair-lattice router** (one logit ψ per C(101,2)=5,050 slice pairs, samples
ONE pair per training step, argmax at eval) → **soft quantizer** (3 trainable
thresholds, annealed sharpness) → patch/transformer trunk → deep head
(256,128,64) → 14 outputs (4 means + 10 Cholesky entries). Loss "v2" is the
tfp-free Gaussian NLL (softplus diagonal, unclipped, batch-SUM). NLLs are only
comparable within a loss dialect; use `val_plain_nll`.

## 6. Traps (each cost real time)

1. Launching the driver without `SMARTPIX_MODEL_NAME` → shallow 1-D router
   (`runs/_o24_wrongarch_cold`).
2. Copying the cold smoothing schedule into a warm start → σ=4 kernel erases
   the inherited incumbent at ep 0 → adjacency trap (`runs/_o24b_smoothtrap`).
   Warm starts must keep the source's σ≈0.
3. `--target N` counts already-converged seeds in the same `--out` dir → one
   output dir per seed.
4. A torn `val_loss` line can freeze the best-weights monitor; always keep
   `last.weights` and evaluate checkpoints directly.
5. `best.weights` is picked on the composite loss unless `SMARTPIX_CKPT_MONITOR`
   is set; the plain-NLL optimum can be lost.
6. Warm-start path does NOT transfer the router unless `PIN_MODE` is
   `inherit`/`inherit_free`.
7. Killing a benchmark's parent without its process group strands worker pools
   (9,781 orphaned `t6.py` procs held 80 GB for 13 days).
8. Bash `setsid … &` wrappers exit immediately — wait on the *log*, not the pid.
